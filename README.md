# Hi, I'm Matt

I'm exploring mathematics, programming, and how to learn independently. I'm drawn to problems I can understand through pictures, mathematical expressions, and formulas.

## How I approach a problem

My first move is usually to simplify. I look for the underlying model, pattern, or structure, break the problem into components I can understand, and connect those components to something I already know. Sometimes that gives me a plausible hypothesis to test. Then I work back up from the simpler pieces toward the original problem.

I see abstract thinking, pattern recognition, and problem-solving as strengths. I also try to examine my own assumptions and notice where my understanding is incomplete. Being able to follow an explanation and being able to develop it myself are different things, and that distinction matters to how I use AI.
